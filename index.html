How We Engage

A four-phase model, no theatre.

Every engagement — whether a 6-week diagnostic or a multi-year platform build — moves through the same disciplined phases. Predictable rhythm, transparent artefacts.

Phase 01

Discover &
Diagnose

Two-to-six week immersion: stakeholder interviews, system archaeology, value-stream mapping. We leave with a quantified opportunity map.

Phase 02

Architect &
Validate
